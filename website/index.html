About

The Dock’s own folders sit on the right. Flotilla lets you group your apps on the left.

  • Grouping doesn’t move your apps or files, and you don’t need to create any folders on disk.

  • Apps, files, Finder folders, and web pages can all share the same group.

  • Click a group icon to open a grid with the same look and animations as the Dock’s built-in folders.

Features

Organize apps, files, and web pages.

Browse folders in the panel

Folders open in the same panel, with file thumbnails. To switch to Finder, choose Open in Finder at the end of the grid.

  • Use the back button to go up one level
  • Turn on Show hidden files in settings

Drag and drop

Drop apps, files, or folders onto a group icon in the Dock or a group in the settings window. You can drop web pages into the settings window too.

Manage groups in the settings window

  • Click Add… to choose apps, files, folders, or web pages
  • Double-click a group to rename it
  • Drag items in the list to reorder them or move them into another group
  • Right-click or Control-click an item for more options

  • Groups alongside your apps

    Each top-level group appears on the left side of the Dock with its own name and icon.

  • Apps, files, and web pages

    Keep apps, files, folders, and web pages together. Files open in their default app, and web pages open in your default browser.

  • Groups within groups

    You can add groups inside other groups. Open a subgroup to browse it, then use the back button to return.

  • Grid view

    Groups open in a grid above the Dock, aligned with their icons.

  • Group icon previews

    The group icon previews the first few items, up to four. You can adjust the number in settings. Its background follows your Mac’s light or dark appearance.

  • Reorder and remove

    Drag items in the panel to reorder them. To remove one, drag it outside, wait for Remove to appear, then let go.

  • Keeps track of your items

    Apps and files still open from a group after you move or rename them. After an app update, Flotilla opens the newly installed version.

  • Runs in the background

    Flotilla runs in the menu bar and stays out of the Dock, even when a panel is open. Panels don’t take focus away from your current app.

  • Five languages

    English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Korean. The app uses your system language, or English if that language isn’t supported.

How it works

How does a group get into the Dock?

To give a group a place in the Dock’s app section, Flotilla creates a placeholder app for each top-level group and adds it with the group’s name and icon.

  1. 1

    Click a group icon

    The Dock launches the placeholder app for that group.

  2. 2

    The placeholder opens a flotilla:// URL

    It uses this URL to notify Flotilla, then quits without bringing another app to the front.

  3. 3

    Flotilla opens or closes the panel

    If Flotilla isn’t running, macOS launches it first. The placeholder also forwards items dropped onto the group icon to Flotilla.

What does Flotilla change on your Mac?

  • Only Flotilla’s group icons

    Flotilla manages group icons through the Dock preferences (com.apple.dock).

    It changes only its own entries in persistent-apps, the group icons on the left side of the Dock. Other icons and Dock settings stay as they are.

  • The Dock restarts after each change

    After a change to the Dock preferences, Flotilla ends the Dock process. macOS automatically restarts it so the change can take effect.

  • Backed up before changes

    Each time Flotilla launches, it exports a full backup of com.apple.dock before making its first change. It keeps the five most recent backups.

    ~/Library/Application Support/Flotilla/Backups/com.apple.dock-<yyyyMMdd-HHmmss>.plist

  • Data and settings

    Data is stored in ~/Library/Application Support/Flotilla:

    • folders.json: the group data
    • DockTiles/: the placeholder apps for top-level groups
    • Backups/: backups of the Dock preferences

    Settings are stored in the preferences domain com.rakuyo.flotilla.

  • Fetching page titles

    Flotilla only connects to the internet to fetch page titles when you add or edit a web page.

FAQ

Installing and using Flotilla

For more details, see the README.

Why does macOS block Flotilla the first time I open it?

Flotilla hasn’t been notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time you try to open it. The alert says Apple cannot verify that it is free of malware.

Click Done, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway under Security. The button is available for an hour after you try to open Flotilla.

See the installation guide →

Can I use Flotilla without Accessibility access?

Yes, with two differences:

  • The panel appears near the pointer and may not line up exactly with the group icon
  • The panel takes a little longer to open after a click
Why might I need to enable Accessibility again after an update?

Because Flotilla uses ad-hoc signing, you may need to enable this permission again after an update.

You can enable it in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility. On macOS 27, go to Privacy & Security › Device Control and Data Access.

Why does the Dock restart?

Flotilla changes the Dock preferences to add or update group icons. It then ends the Dock process, and macOS automatically restarts it so the change can take effect.

Group previews and switches between light and dark appearance also update the icons, so they trigger a Dock restart too.

How do I remove a group icon from the Dock and add it back?

Drag the group icon out of the Dock to remove it.

The group stays in the settings window, marked Not in Dock, and won’t return to the Dock automatically. To bring the icon back, right-click the group and choose Add to Dock.

Which Macs does Flotilla run on?

Flotilla runs on Macs with Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later.

Which languages does Flotilla support?

English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Korean. The app uses your system language, or English if that language isn’t supported.

The app’s name follows your system language too: 归帆 in Simplified Chinese, 歸帆 in Traditional Chinese, 帰帆 in Japanese, and Flotilla in other languages.
