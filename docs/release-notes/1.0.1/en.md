### New

- When an update is available, the update window shows what's new in that version in Flotilla's language

### Improved

- Finder folders now look the way they do in Finder, with their tag color, emoji, or symbol. This applies in the panel, the settings window, group icons, and subfolders opened in the panel, and changes show up right away
- **Version History** in the **You’re up to date!** alert now opens the release notes on the official website

### Fixed

- Clicking an app in a group did nothing if the app had quit but left a background process running. Apps in a group are no longer replaced by another process of the same app running elsewhere, and open just as they do from the Dock or Finder
