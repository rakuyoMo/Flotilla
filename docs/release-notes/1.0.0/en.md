### New

- Groups sit on the left side of the Dock alongside your apps, each with its own name and an icon that previews up to four of its items
- A group can hold apps, files, folders, web pages, and subgroups
- Click a group to open a grid above the Dock, with the same look and animations as the Dock's built-in folders
- Folders open in the same panel with file thumbnails, and the grid includes **Open in Finder**
- Drop apps, files, or folders onto a group in the Dock or in the settings window to add them; web pages can be dropped into the settings window
- Drag items in the panel to reorder them; to remove one, drag it outside, wait for **Remove** to appear, then let go
- Apps and files still open from a group after you move or rename them, and after an app update Flotilla opens the newly installed version
- Flotilla runs in the menu bar and stays out of the Dock, and panels don't take focus away from your current app
- Checks GitHub Releases for updates daily or with **Check for Updates…**, and can download updates automatically and install them on quit
- Available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Korean
